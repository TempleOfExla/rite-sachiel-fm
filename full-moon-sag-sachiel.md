## Sachiel / Jupiter in Cancer Ceremony

### Timing

**Primary window**

- Sagittarius Full Moon: May 31, 2026 at 1:45am PT
- Practical timing: Friday evening May 30 (Gordon's approach — don't do it at 3am)

**Backup window**

- Any Thursday at sunrise while Jupiter is in Cancer (Jupiter stays in Cancer through mid-2026)
- Must begin at actual sunrise, not whenever you wake up

---

### Materia

- 2 blue candles (for Sachiel)
- 2 white candles (for the moon)
- Seal of Sachiel drawn in blue, purple, or gold
- Offering bowl with blue offerings (Gordon used blue candy; work with what you have)
- Frankincense incense
- Rosary, mala, or beads — optional, for the affirmation phase

---

### Structure

**1. Open**

However feels right for you personally:

- Shamanic opening
- Lord's Prayer
- Calling in divine white light

The point is sanctifying the space, not the specific method.

**2. Orphic Hymn to the Moon**

Gordon recommends avoiding the Thomas Taylor translations — generate a fresh one via AI if needed, or find an alternative translation.

**3. Orphic Hymn to Sachiel**

*O Sachiel, Radiant One, Keeper of the Benevolent Flame,*
*You who wear the mantle of the sky's wide order,*
*Whose breath is the spring wind that awakens all things —*
*Come now to this place,*
*Where the moon is full and the heart is open.*

*Blesséd are you, who gives freely and asks only readiness.*
*You stir the wise to speak, the wanderer to walk,*
*You plant visions in the minds of those*
*Who dare to ask for more than safety.*

*Let your light fall upon this threshold.*
*Not as blinding glory, but as the golden hush*
*That precedes discovery.*
*Let your signs be many, and your silence be brief.*

*Bring teachers with eyes like lanterns.*
*Bring companions whose laughter rings true.*
*Bring trials that shape but do not break.*
*Bring roads that are longer than maps allow.*

*O Prince of Peaceful Power,*
*Crowned in amethyst, clothed in thunder,*
*Pour your blessings into this willing vessel.*
*Make of me a pilgrim who finds what he seeks —*
*And more, always more.*

*So be it,*
*As the stars are many*
*And the path is long.*

**4. Make Offerings**

Place blue offerings in the bowl. Light frankincense.

**5. Sit in Presence**

Spend time quietly in the space — candles, incense, nighttime. Practice *noticing*. You may receive something, you may just absorb the atmosphere. Both are fine.

**6. Florence Scovel Shinn Affirmation**

Use beads if you have them. Repeat multiple times — don't just say it once.

> *"New fields of divine activity now open for me. Unexpected doors fly open. Unexpected channels are free."*

**7. Close**

Thank Sachiel — Gordon frames this like saying goodbye to a guest. License to depart. You can blow out the candles.

---

### Ongoing Practice

- Add Sachiel's seal to your altar if you have one
- Light a candle on Thursday mornings occasionally
- Practice ongoing noticing — watch for synchronicities along Jupiter-in-Cancer themes: home, nourishment, expansion, abundance, meaningful travel

---

### The Goal

Not to petition for specific things, but to signal conscious alignment with the Jupiter in Cancer current — to become an intentional co-expresser of what the cosmos is already doing.
